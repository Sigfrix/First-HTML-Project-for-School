Who we are

Welcome to the Ghibli Gallery, a fan-made website celebrating the enchanting worlds of Studio Ghibli.

This gallery was created to showcase the films, characters, artwork, and stories that have made Studio Ghibli beloved by audiences around the world. From breathtaking landscapes to unforgettable characters, each film carries its own unique atmosphere and message.

Explore the gallery, discover different Ghibli films, and take a journey through worlds filled with imagination, adventure, friendship, and wonder.

This website is an educational/fan project and is not officially affiliated with Studio Ghibli.
